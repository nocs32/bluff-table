# Sounds

The game's sounds come from [Freesound](https://freesound.org) under **CC0** (public domain), so no credit is required. They're credited here anyway.

| File | Source | Author | What was done to it |
|---|---|---|---|
| `chime.wav` | [Bell Chime Alert](https://freesound.org/people/plasterbrain/sounds/419493/) | plasterbrain | left channel only (the two channels partly cancel when mixed to mono), silence trimmed from the start, the cut-off tail faded out over 0.2 s, evened out in level |
| `slap.wav` | [cardDrop2.wav](https://freesound.org/people/deathpie/sounds/19244/) | deathpie | the second hit alone (the card snapping down), 0.08 s from 0.30 s, cut below 120 Hz, faded out, evened out in level |
| `deal.wav` | [Card Deal](https://freesound.org/people/RealSquink/sounds/787405/) | RealSquink | the slide and the contact, 0.38 s from 0.18 s, cut below 150 Hz, faded in and out, evened out in level |
| `shuffle.wav` | [Riffle Card Shuffle](https://freesound.org/people/Kodack/sounds/256508/) | Kodack | the riffle, 1.47 s from 0.28 s, cut below 120 Hz, faded out, evened out in level |
| `fuse.wav` | [fuse_burning_48khz.wav](https://freesound.org/people/elonen/sounds/45650/) | elonen | 2 s of the steady hiss from 2.0 s, cut below 200 Hz, looped with a 0.2 s crossfade, kept quieter than the others |
| `creak.wav` | [0005_Crystal Lamp with Swing Front.wav](https://freesound.org/people/Andreas.Mustola/sounds/84526/) | Andreas.Mustola | a chandelier swinging on its chain, 2.2 s from 0.2 s, cut below 100 Hz, faded out, evened out in level |

All of them are mono, 16-bit, 48 kHz, cut from Freesound's high-quality previews.
